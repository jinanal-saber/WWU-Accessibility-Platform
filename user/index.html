HOW IT WORKS

Share what campus navigation is really like.

This interactive map brings together WWU’s existing accessibility information with experiences shared by the WWU community. Report barriers you encounter, find accessibility features around campus, track the status of reported issues, and stay informed about accessibility concerns that may affect you.

Your experience matters. Even something that seems small, like a difficult entrance, blocked pathway, broken elevator, or an area that wasn’t designed with accessibility in mind, can affect how someone gets around campus.

Share an accessibility issue you encounter on campus and pinpoint its location on the map.

TRACK A REPORT

Follow reported issues to see their current status and whether they are being addressed or have been resolved.

STAY INFORMED

Sign up for email notifications about accessibility issues reported around campus that may affect you.
